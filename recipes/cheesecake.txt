Cheesecake
==============

A decadent cheesecake for a special occasion.

Ingredients:
- 1 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cups sugar
- 2 cups cream cheesecake
- 1 tin of condensed milk
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup of butter
- 2 cups crushed biscuits

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 175 degrees C (350 degrees F)
2. Mix all wet ingredients in a large bowl
4. Mix the butter and biscuits and build the base
5. Pour into 1 greased 9-inch round pans
6. Bake for 35 minutes until the top starts to brown

Tips:
- Wait for it to chill for it to properly set
